What's on in December

Free events for all ages at Quillmoor Library. Most need no booking. For the two that do, save a place with the form further down.

Events

  • Seed swap in the reading garden

    Saturday 5 December, 10:00 to 12:00

    Bring seeds you saved this year and take home someone else's. Envelopes and pencils are on the table by the gate.

    Reading garden Just turn up

  • Knit and natter

    Tuesday 8 December, 14:00 to 16:00

    Bring whatever you are making, or learn to cast on. Spare needles and wool live in the basket by the window.

    Upstairs room Just turn up

  • Homework club

    Thursday 10 December, 16:00 to 17:30

    For ages 9 to 14. Volunteers help with reading, maths and projects. Places are limited, so save one first.

    Study room Save a place

  • Winter reading challenge launch

    Saturday 12 December, 11:00

    Read six books before February and collect a stamp for each one. Share your favourites with #QuillmoorWinterReadingChallenge.

    Children's corner Just turn up

  • History walk along the old mill race

    Saturday 19 December, 13:30 to 15:00

    A gentle walk with Odile Varga of the Quillmoor History Society. About two miles on paths that can be muddy, so wear boots.
