Kilnworth Pottery

We make mugs, bowls and plates by hand at 14 Saddler Street, and we teach anyone who wants to learn.

Come in and say hello. The kettle is usually on.

Classes Kofi teaches two classes a week. On Thursday evenings you learn to throw pots on the wheel. On Saturday mornings you build them by hand, with no wheel at all. Wear clothes you do not mind getting muddy. The shop Everything on our shelves was made here in the studio. Mugs, bowls and plates change with every firing, so come back often. The kiln is opened on Friday nights, so the shelves are fullest on a Saturday. Visit us 14 Saddler Street Ostley OS4 7RB Open Tuesday to Saturday, 10am to 5pm.
