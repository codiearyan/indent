Classes

Kofi teaches two classes a week. On Thursday evenings you learn to throw pots on the wheel. On Saturday mornings you build them by hand, with no wheel at all.

Wear clothes you do not mind getting muddy.

What to bring:

  • an apron or an old shirt
  • a small towel
  • a hair tie
  • a bag for wet clay

Your first wheel class goes like this:

  1. Wedge the clay to push the air out.
  2. Centre it on the wheel.
  3. Open it up and pull up the walls.
  4. Cut it off the wheel and leave it to dry.

Times and prices are on the classes page.
